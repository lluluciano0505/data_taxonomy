COMMUNITY WORKSHOP MINUTES — SYNTHETIC DEMO

Date: 18 September 2025 (fictional)
Session: Cluster B public-space and continuity workshop
Participants: fictional resident representatives, youth workers, housing association liaison, municipal design lead, and accessibility adviser.

KEY THEMES

Residents asked the team to repair entrances before adding new buildings, keep a clear route to the metro connection, protect mature trees, and explain construction disruption in plain language.
