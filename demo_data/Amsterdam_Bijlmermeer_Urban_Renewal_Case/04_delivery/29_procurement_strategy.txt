PROCUREMENT STRATEGY — SYNTHETIC DISCUSSION PAPER

The case compares a traditional design-bid-build route for the public-realm pilot with an early-contractor-involvement route for the tower retrofit. A framework call-off may reduce mobilisation time but must not weaken resident continuity, transparent scope definition, or independent safety review.

RECOMMENDATION FOR TESTING

Use a small enabling-works package for surveys and temporary access, followed by a two-stage route for the retrofit pilot. Keep the blue-green maintenance requirements in the contract rather than treating planting as a handover-only item.
