PUBLIC REALM AND EVERYDAY SAFETY STRATEGY

The public-realm concept converts the current movement hierarchy into a connected sequence of front doors, play spaces, rain gardens, cycle routes, and overlooked seating areas. The primary route follows the existing green structure but introduces clearer crossings, lighting, and addresses at tower entrances.

TARGETS FOR THE SYNTHETIC OPTION

- 1.2 km accessible blue-green connector.
- 12 new threshold spaces at tower and community entrances.
- 4 rain gardens with an indicative combined storage volume of 480 cubic metres.
- 3 play and social spaces designed with residents.
- 400 m maximum walking distance from any residential entrance to a transit connection.

The strategy deliberately links landscape, mobility, social infrastructure, and water management so Layer 2 can detect more than one plausible domain while retaining a primary domain of Landscape & Public Realm.
