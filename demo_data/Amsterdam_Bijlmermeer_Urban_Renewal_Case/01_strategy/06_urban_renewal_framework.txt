URBAN RENEWAL FRAMEWORK — CONCEPT OPTION C

The synthetic framework organises renewal around four linked moves: retain and repair viable towers; reconnect movement at ground level; strengthen the landscape as blue-green infrastructure; and add active, mixed-use edges where parking and service areas currently create dead frontage.

SPATIAL PRINCIPLES

1. Keep the largest continuous green rooms and improve their edges rather than fragmenting them.
2. Make a legible, step-free route from each tower entrance to the transit stop and neighbourhood centre.
3. Place new homes and community uses on streets so that daily activity supports passive observation.
4. Use water storage, shade, and biodiversity as visible public-space functions.
5. Treat memory, resident continuity, and affordability as design requirements rather than consultation afterthoughts.

DECISION GATE

At the end of concept stage, the team must select whether the first package is a tower retrofit pilot, a public-realm and drainage pilot, or a combined package. The evidence chain should cite the structural condition survey, flood assessment, demographic profile, cost plan, and community minutes.

This document is synthetic and is intended to be classified as an urban design / planning strategy with high information value and a whole-project scope.
