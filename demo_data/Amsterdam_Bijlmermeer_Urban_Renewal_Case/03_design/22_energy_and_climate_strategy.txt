ENERGY AND CLIMATE STRATEGY

The hypothetical climate strategy compares fabric-first retrofit, connection to a low-temperature district network, and all-electric replacement systems. The preferred concept is to reduce heat demand before selecting plant, retain viable structures to avoid unnecessary embodied carbon, and use shaded public routes to reduce heat stress.

ILLUSTRATIVE TARGETS

Reduce modelled space-heating demand by 35 percent in the pilot tower; provide 70 percent of new roof area as photovoltaic-ready; and increase shaded seating along the primary route. These are option-testing targets, not a performance guarantee.

The strategy must be coordinated with the structural condition survey, housing inventory, cost plan, and resident continuity requirements.
