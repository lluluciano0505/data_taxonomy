LANDSCAPE AND ECOLOGY STRATEGY

The synthetic ecology strategy treats the Bijlmermeer landscape as infrastructure: canopy cools routes, rain gardens store water, meadow edges support pollinators, and clear thresholds make large green spaces feel public and cared for.

TARGET HABITATS

A wet meadow basin, shade tree allee, native shrub edge, and pollinator strips are proposed as a connected sequence. Existing mature trees are retained where root zones and access requirements permit. Lighting is kept low and directional near habitat edges.

The strategy is a concept recommendation, not an ecological survey. Species lists, protected habitat status, maintenance budgets, and seasonal construction restrictions require specialist confirmation. Its primary decision is where the blue-green spine should be protected from later building and utility claims.
