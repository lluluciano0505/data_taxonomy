MOBILITY AND TRANSIT STRATEGY

The case proposes a movement hierarchy that gives priority to walking, cycling, accessible transit transfer, emergency access, and then servicing. The old separation between elevated movement and ground-level streets is treated as a coordination condition rather than something to erase in one phase.

ILLUSTRATIVE MOVES

- Create a 4.0 metre shared mobility corridor at the main desire line.
- Add two raised crossings at the service-road interface.
- Consolidate deliveries into a timed east-court bay.
- Keep emergency access clear while reducing through-traffic.
- Improve wayfinding between the metro connection, community hub, and tower entrances.

The strategy should be tested against the transport counts, noise assessment, phasing plan, and resident safety map before the route is fixed.
