HERITAGE AND MEMORY NOTE

This synthetic note does not assign a formal heritage designation. It identifies memory-sensitive places and narratives that should remain visible during renewal: the scale of the original green structure, the history of high-rise living, resident-led adaptation, and the memory of the 1992 cargo-plane disaster.

The design team should avoid turning memory into a single monument or marketing theme. The concept proposes a distributed approach: retain selected landscape alignments, record resident stories through a consent-based process, use clear interpretation at the community hub, and protect the dignity of affected communities.

Before formal use, consult authoritative archives and community representatives. All names, dates, and proposed interventions in this file are placeholders for the demonstration.
