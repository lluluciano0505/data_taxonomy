SPATIAL FRAMEWORK — CONCEPT PLAN NARRATIVE

The synthetic spatial framework describes a three-part structure: retained residential towers as vertical neighbourhood markers; a blue-green spine as the shared civic landscape; and active street edges as the interface for new housing, services, and everyday mobility.

The framework keeps the largest existing tree rooms, introduces a clear address at each entrance, and places new uses at the boundary between parking courts and public streets. The main route is designed as a step-free, lit, overlooked connection rather than a decorative landscape path.

The plan is deliberately at concept scale. It does not establish a cadastral boundary, statutory designation, or buildable envelope. Its value is to connect evidence from housing, flood, mobility, ecology, and community files into a decision about spatial structure.
