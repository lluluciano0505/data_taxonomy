SYNTHETIC DEMO — illustrative training case, not an authentic project record.


CASE OVERVIEW

This synthetic case follows a hypothetical 28-hectare urban renewal study in Amsterdam Zuidoost, focused on a representative Bijlmermeer superblock called Cluster B. The learning question is how an archive of planning, social, technical, and delivery documents can be classified before a design team decides what to read first.

The case uses real public themes associated with Bijlmermeer: post-war high-rise planning, elevated movement systems, large green spaces, later renewal, housing diversification, and the memory of the 1992 cargo-plane disaster. The project name, boundaries, figures, dates, organisations, survey results, and decisions below are invented for this DataTaxonomy demonstration.

SYNTHETIC PROJECT THESIS

The hypothetical team proposes a repair-and-reconnect strategy rather than wholesale clearance. Existing tower blocks are assessed for selective retrofit; parking and service edges become active streets; a continuous rain garden and ecological corridor links neighbourhood spaces; and new mid-rise housing adds tenure diversity without displacing current residents.

CORE PROJECT QUESTIONS

1. Which existing structures can be retained safely and economically?
2. How should pedestrian, bicycle, bus, and service movement be reorganised around safer street edges?
3. How can flood storage, shade, biodiversity, and everyday public life share the green spine?
4. Which decisions are statutory or physically binding, and which are advisory?
5. Which files should a design lead read first during Concept / Schematic stage?

DEMO SETUP

The 37 files are deliberately mixed: narrative documents, tables, spatial data, a simplified coordination model, visual material, meeting records, and delivery controls. The expected output is a searchable CSV with domain, scale, lifecycle, asset type, governance, confidentiality, certainty, and decision-priority fields. This is a synthetic training case, not a planning submission or historical archive.
